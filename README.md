<p>The Polarity logo is inspired by an [arctic hare](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arctic_hare) which has a white winter coat and a dark summer coat. The fur pattern resembles the yin and yang symbol, playing on the duality between data and codata.</p><br>
<p>The logo was designed by Hendrik Süberkrüb.</p><br>
<p>The design is intended to reflect the Arctic, maintaining the polar theme of the language.</p>
